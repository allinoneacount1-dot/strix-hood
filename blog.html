Editorial

Three rules for anything published here

Rule 01

Show the alternative we rejected

A design post without the discarded options is an advertisement. Every decision piece names what was tried first and why it failed, which is why several of the outlines above end on the thing the design does not solve.

Rule 02

Postmortems are blameless and specific

When there is one to write: named commitments we missed, real timestamps, and the diff that fixed it. If the interesting failure is organisational rather than technical, we say that instead of hiding behind a race condition. There are none yet, and we are not writing fiction to fill the section.

Rule 03

No launch posts

Product changes go in the changelog and the docs. This is for reasoning that outlives the release it came from — if a post would be worthless in a year, it does not get written.

Subscribe

One email when the first one lands

New posts only, and there are none yet — so this list is silent until there are. Incident notices live on the status page and have their own list, so subscribing here will never get you a product announcement.

Disagree in public.

Every outline above started as an argument inside the team, and the useful ones get settled by someone outside it. The fastest way to change our mind — or the shape of a piece before it is written — is a specific counter-example.
